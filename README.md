## About

Hack the Heart explores connections between people through conversations between AI representations of them. It starts with contacts collected on Hack the North badges and profiles based on approved answers and available writing samples.

## How it works

Import badge contacts, review the preview, and choose what to save. Agents representing two people then exchange six messages. You can inspect their conversation and its analysis before deciding whether to meet.

Four specialized roles interpret messages, capture writing style, compose replies, and incorporate feedback from real dates. Profile facts stay separate from temporary observations.

Compatibility is calculated in code from structured assessments of compatibility, friction, reciprocity, pacing, and connection. The result describes the simulated exchange; it is not a prediction of relationship success.

## Built with

Next.js, React, TypeScript, Python, FastAPI, MongoDB, OpenAI, Apify, Zod, and Vercel Workflow.
